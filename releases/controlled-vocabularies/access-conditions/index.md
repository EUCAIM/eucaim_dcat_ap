# EUCAIM Access Conditions

The EUCAIM Access Conditions controlled vocabulary describes the conditions or
modes under which access to an EUCAIM Dataset Distribution is provided.

- [Download the vocabulary in Turtle](access-conditions.ttl)
- [Download the vocabulary as CSV](access-conditions.csv)

## Concepts

### Download access {#DOWNLOAD}

**Notation:** `DOWNLOAD`

The data may be downloaded by an authorised user, subject to the applicable
access procedure, policy, and legal conditions.

**Concept IRI:**

`https://eucaim.github.io/eucaim_dcat_ap/releases/controlled-vocabularies/access-conditions/#DOWNLOAD`

### In-situ processing {#IN_SITU_PROCESSING}

**Notation:** `IN_SITU_PROCESSING`

Authorised users may access, view, and process the data within the secure
environment in which the data are made available.

**Concept IRI:**

`https://eucaim.github.io/eucaim_dcat_ap/releases/controlled-vocabularies/access-conditions/#IN_SITU_PROCESSING`

### Remote processing without direct data access {#REMOTE_PROCESSING}

**Notation:** `REMOTE_PROCESSING`

Authorised processing may be submitted to the environment holding the data,
without providing the user with direct access to the underlying data.

**Concept IRI:**

`https://eucaim.github.io/eucaim_dcat_ap/releases/controlled-vocabularies/access-conditions/#REMOTE_PROCESSING`
